# HYDRA SAMO — CINEMATIC VISUAL ASSET SYSTEM

You are not creating a generic portfolio website.

You are creating the **visual asset library and motion-design direction for HYDRA SAMO**, a creative studio/artist identity focused on:

**Video Editing · Motion Design · Voice Over · Graphic Design**

The website must feel like an **award-level cinematic creative studio**, not a SaaS website, template, agency dashboard, WebGL demo, or generic Awwwards clone.

The visual identity is already established. **Do not redesign the brand. Do not invent a different identity.**

## 01 — BRAND DNA

HYDRA SAMO is represented by an abstract three-part logo.

The meaning is:

**THREE → ONE**

Three independent visual forces eventually resolve into one identity.

IMPORTANT:

* Do NOT create a literal hydra.
* Do NOT create animal heads.
* Do NOT create tentacles.
* Do NOT create plants.
* Do NOT create organic vines.
* Do NOT create monsters.
* Do NOT create fantasy creatures.
* Do NOT make the logo float around the website.
* Do NOT repeatedly display the complete logo as decoration.
* Do NOT create shattered logo fragments.
* Do NOT create floating 3D logo pieces.
* Do NOT create obvious copies of the logo.

The logo geometry should influence the **composition, curves, direction, lighting and spatial relationships**, but the viewer should not constantly see the logo.

The identity should be **felt rather than literally illustrated.**
